Local search & Google Business Profile

Built to support Miami visibility without inventing local signals.

The website uses consistent business identity, service language and location context that can be aligned with a verified Google Business Profile. Replace the owner-supplied placeholders with the exact real-world profile information before launch.

Miami service relevance

Core pages clearly state the Miami market while keeping broader service availability separate. Add real service-area pages only for places the business genuinely serves.

GBP alignment

Keep the business name, phone, URL, categories, hours and service descriptions consistent with the verified profile. Add the real GBP profile URL and street address only if they are public and accurate.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for prospects and answer engines.

What does Mairket do?

Mairket combines web design, SEO, AEO/GEO, paid media, content, AI voice agents, chatbots and automation into coordinated growth systems.

Where is Mairket based?

Mairket is based in Miami, Florida and can work with businesses outside the local market as well.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO improves discoverability in search engines. AEO makes content easier for answer systems to extract and present. GEO focuses on making a brand, its entities and its expertise clearer and more citeable in generative AI search experiences.

Can the website support Google Business Profile optimization?

Yes. The site can reinforce the same real business name, services, categories, phone, hours, location and topical relevance used in a verified Google Business Profile. Those facts should be supplied by the business owner and kept consistent.

Can OpenClaw edit this template?
